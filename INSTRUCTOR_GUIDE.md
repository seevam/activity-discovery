# Identity Collage Builder - Instructor Guide

## 📋 Activity Overview

The **Identity Collage Builder** is a digital activity that helps middle school students (grades 6-8) create a visual representation of their identity, interests, values, and career aspirations. This interactive tool combines creative expression with career exploration, allowing students to reflect on who they are while earning badges and completing challenges.

**Platform:** Ascend Now Career Exploration Platform
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Format:** Individual, self-paced activity
**Best Used:** After Session 1 career exploration activities

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this activity, students will:

1. **Self-Reflect**: Identify and articulate personal strengths, values, and interests
2. **Visual Thinking**: Express abstract concepts through visual representation
3. **Career Awareness**: Connect personal attributes to career pathways
4. **Digital Literacy**: Use digital tools for creative expression
5. **Goal Setting**: Envision and articulate future aspirations

---

## ⏱️ Time Breakdown

| Phase | Duration | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Session 1 Input | 2-3 minutes | Enter themes, interests, and career clusters |
| Template Selection | 1 minute | Choose a design layout |
| Builder (5 Challenges) | 10-12 minutes | Create collage with images, text, and stickers |
| About Me Statement | 2-3 minutes | Write a personal reflection |

**Total: 15-20 minutes**

## 📚 Step-by-Step Instructions

### **Phase 1: Getting Started (Session 1 Input)**

**What Students Do:**
1. Click "Let's Get Started!" on the welcome page
2. Enter information from their Session 1 career discovery:
   - **Themes** (e.g., "Creative," "Helping Others," "Technology")
   - **Interests** (e.g., "Art," "Sports," "Music")
   - **Career Clusters** (e.g., "Arts & Communication," "STEM," "Health Sciences")
3. Click "Continue to Template Selection"

**Instructor Tips:**
- Ensure students have completed Session 1 activities first
- Have students reference their Session 1 worksheets/notes
- Encourage selecting 3-5 items per category for best results
- Remind them: This information personalizes their experience

---

### **Phase 2: Template Selection**

**What Students Do:**
1. Browse 5 different template layouts:
   - **Grid Layout**: Organized sections for different elements
   - **Circular Flow**: Radial design emanating from center
   - **Freeform Canvas**: No structure, complete creative freedom
   - **Blank Canvas**: Start from scratch
   - **Prefilled Sample**: Example collage for inspiration
2. Select their preferred template
3. Click "Start Building"

**Instructor Tips:**
- **Grid Layout** works well for organized students
- **Freeform** is great for creative, artistic students
- Show the prefilled example if students need inspiration
- Templates only provide structure; content is fully customizable

---

### **Phase 3: The Builder - 5 Challenges**

This is the main activity where students create their collage by completing 5 challenges.

#### **Challenge 1: Show Your Strengths**
- **Goal:** Add 3+ images that represent their strengths
- **Tools Available:**
  - 🔍 **Search Images**: Find photos from Unsplash
  - 🎨 **AI Generate**: Create custom images with AI
  - 📤 **Upload Photo**: Use personal photos
  - ✏️ **Add Text**: Label strengths with text

**Instructor Tips:**
- Encourage students to think beyond academic strengths
- Examples: "I'm a good listener," "I'm creative," "I solve problems"
- Images can be literal or metaphorical

#### **Challenge 2: Your Values**
- **Goal:** Add 2+ elements representing their values
- **Examples of Values:**
  - Honesty, Kindness, Teamwork, Independence
  - Family, Friendship, Achievement, Adventure
  - Creativity, Justice, Learning, Nature

**Instructor Tips:**
- Help students distinguish values from interests
- Values = what matters most to them, their guiding principles
- Use text tool for value words, images for visual representation

#### **Challenge 3: Your Words to Live By**
- **Goal:** Add an inspiring quote or personal motto
- **Three Options:**
  1. **Choose from Suggested Quotes** (5 personalized quotes)
  2. **Browse Quote Library** (60+ quotes in 9 categories)
  3. **Write Your Own** (custom text)

**Quote Library Categories:**
- 💪 Motivation | 🦁 Courage | 🎨 Creativity
- ⭐ Be Yourself | 📚 Learning | 💝 Kindness
- 👑 Leadership | 🤝 Teamwork | 🔥 Never Give Up

**Instructor Tips:**
- Students can customize fonts, colors, and effects
- Encourage selecting quotes that truly resonate
- Writing their own motto is often most meaningful
- They can preview before adding to canvas

#### **Challenge 4: Colors & Symbols**
- **Goal:** Add 2+ visual elements that represent them
- **Ideas:**
  - Favorite colors as backgrounds
  - Symbols (hearts, stars, arrows)
  - Shapes that have meaning
  - Patterns that reflect personality

**Instructor Tips:**
- This is the most abstract challenge
- Encourage creativity and personal interpretation
- Use stickers (unlocked from badges) for symbols
- Layer elements for artistic effects

#### **Challenge 5: Your Future Vision**
- **Goal:** Add 2+ images about future goals and dreams
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Career aspirations
  - Places they want to visit
  - Skills they want to develop
  - Impact they want to make

**Instructor Tips:**
- Help students think 5-10 years ahead
- Dreams can be specific or general
- Encourage both realistic and aspirational goals

### **Builder Tools Explained**

#### **🔍 Search Images**
- Searches Unsplash's library of high-quality photos
- Students type keywords (e.g., "mountain," "teamwork," "science")
- Click images to add to canvas
- Free to use, properly licensed

#### **🎨 AI Generate**
- Creates custom images using AI (DALL-E)
- Students describe what they want
- Choose style: Icon, Illustration, Abstract, Realistic
- Limited credits (10 per session)

#### **📤 Upload Photo**
- Upload personal photos from device
- Must be appropriate for school
- Good for family photos, artwork, etc.
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG

#### **✏️ Add Text**
- Add quotes, labels, or words
- Customize:
  - **7 font styles** (handwriting, bold, elegant, etc.)
  - **Font size** (12-72pt)
  - **Color picker** (any color)
  - **Effects**: Bold, Italic, Shadow
- Preview before adding

#### **✨ Stickers**
- Unlocked by earning badges
- Fun decorative elements
- Drag and drop onto canvas

#### **Canvas Controls**
- ↶ **Undo/Redo**: Fix mistakes
- ↑↓ **Layer Controls**: Move elements forward/back
- 🗑️ **Delete**: Remove selected element
- **Drag**: Move elements around
- **Resize**: Click and drag corners
- **Rotate**: Rotation handle when selected

---

### **Badge System**

Students earn badges by completing challenges:

1. **Explorer Badge** → Challenge 1 complete → Unlocks nature stickers
2. **Values Champion** → Challenge 2 complete → Unlocks heart stickers
3. **Wordsmith** → Challenge 3 complete → Unlocks star stickers
4. **Artist** → Challenge 4 complete → Unlocks artistic stickers
5. **Visionary** → Challenge 5 complete → Unlocks achievement stickers
6. **Master Storyteller** → All challenges complete (final badge)

**Instructor Tips:**
- Badges provide motivation and milestones
- New stickers unlock after each badge
- Celebrate when students earn all 6 badges!

---

### **Phase 4: About Me Statement**

**What Students Do:**
1. Write 50-100 words answering:
   - Who are you?
   - What do you love?
   - What makes you unique?
   - Where do you want to go?
2. **Optional AI Help**: Click "Get AI Writing Suggestions"
   - Receive 3 starter paragraphs (Creative, Collaborative, Builder focus)
   - Use as-is or edit to make it their own
3. Click "I'm Done!" when finished

**Instructor Tips:**
- This synthesizes their entire collage
- Encourage first-person writing ("I am...")
- AI suggestions are starting points, not final answers
- Should reflect their authentic voice
- Word count shows progress (minimum 50 characters)

---

### **Phase 5: Review & Download**

**What Students See:**
- Their completed collage
- Achievement stats (challenges, badges, elements)
- Badge collection with descriptions
- Their "About Me" statement

**Download Options:**
1. **PDF (Printable)**
   - Full collage + badges + About Me
   - Modern, artistic layout
   - Professional format for portfolios
   - Perfect for printing and sharing

2. **PNG (Digital)**
   - Just the collage image
   - High quality
   - Easy to share digitally

**Instructor Tips:**
- Have students download both formats
- PDF is great for parent conferences
- PNG can be shared on school platforms
- Can be included in digital portfolios

## 🎓 Facilitation Strategies

### **Before the Activity**

1. **Complete Session 1 First**
   - Students need their themes, interests, and career clusters
   - Have worksheets/notes ready to reference

2. **Set Up Technology**
   - Test internet connection
   - Ensure browser compatibility (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
   - Check that image search and uploads work

3. **Establish Guidelines**
   - All content must be school-appropriate
   - Images should be respectful and relevant
   - Personal photos should not reveal private information

4. **Frame the Purpose**
   - "This is YOUR story - make it authentic"
   - "No two collages will look the same"
   - "Focus on what matters to you, not what looks 'cool'"

### **During the Activity**

1. **Circulate and Support**
   - Help students who are stuck
   - Ask guiding questions: "What are you proud of?" "What makes you happy?"
   - Encourage risk-taking and creativity

2. **Manage Time**
   - Give 2-minute warnings before transitions
   - Most students finish in 15-20 minutes
   - Some may need extra time on About Me

3. **Celebrate Effort**
   - Recognize when students earn badges
   - Highlight creative or thoughtful choices
   - Encourage peer sharing (if appropriate)

4. **Technical Troubleshooting**
   - Image won't load? → Try different search terms
   - Can't move element? → Click to select first
   - Lost work? → Auto-saves every 30 seconds!

### **After the Activity**

1. **Reflection Questions**
   - What was most challenging to represent visually?
   - What did you learn about yourself?
   - How do your values connect to your career interests?
   - What surprised you about your collage?

2. **Gallery Walk** (Optional)
   - Students view each other's collages
   - Share 1-2 highlights verbally
   - Celebrate diversity and creativity

3. **Next Steps**
   - Connect collages to Session 2 activities
   - Use in career counseling conversations
   - Include in student portfolios
   - Share with parents/guardians

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Guide

### **Common Issues**

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **"I can't find good images"** | Try different keywords; use AI generator; upload own photos |
| **"My canvas is too crowded"** | Delete elements using 🗑️; use layering to organize; less can be more |
| **"I don't know what quote to choose"** | Browse quote library by category; relate to personal experiences; can write own |
| **"The element won't move"** | Click to select it first (handles appear); then drag |
| **"I accidentally deleted something"** | Use ↶ Undo button immediately |
| **"My work isn't saving"** | Check internet connection; look for "✓ Saved" in header |
| **"AI image generator isn't working"** | Credits may be used up (10 total); use search or upload instead |
| **"Can I edit after downloading?"** | Yes! Return to builder anytime; changes auto-save |

### **Technical Requirements**

- **Internet Connection**: Required (no offline mode)
- **Browsers**: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge (latest versions)
- **Devices**: Desktop, laptop, tablet, Chromebook
- **Screen Size**: Works best on screens 10" or larger

## 📊 Assessment & Evaluation

### **Completion Criteria**

✅ All 5 challenges completed
✅ Minimum 8-10 elements on canvas
✅ About Me statement (50+ words)
✅ Collage downloaded

### **Quality Indicators**

**Excellent Collage:**
- Thoughtful element selection
- Personal and authentic
- Clearly reflects stated themes/values
- Visually balanced and organized
- About Me connects to visual elements

**Developing Collage:**
- Generic or random elements
- Limited personal connection
- Cluttered or empty canvas
- About Me doesn't match visuals
- Minimal effort evident

### **Reflection Prompts**

Use these to assess deeper understanding:

1. **Self-Awareness**
   - "Describe how your collage represents who you are"
   - "Which element is most important to you? Why?"

2. **Values Clarification**
   - "How do your values influence your interests?"
   - "Which value was hardest to represent? Why?"

3. **Career Connection**
   - "How do your strengths connect to your career interests?"
   - "What skills do you need to develop for your future vision?"

4. **Growth Mindset**
   - "What did you include about your future self?"
   - "How might your collage change in 5 years?"

## 💡 Differentiation Strategies

### **For Struggling Students**

- **Use prefilled template** as starting point
- **Provide specific prompts**: "Add an image of something that makes you happy"
- **Offer word banks** for values and strengths
- **Allow partner discussion** before creating
- **Extend time** if needed
- **Use AI suggestions** for About Me

### **For Advanced Students**

- **Choose freeform canvas** for more creativity
- **Challenge**: Create collage telling a story (beginning → end)
- **Use all tools**: Search, AI, upload, text
- **Write completely original quote**
- **Add artistic effects**: Layering, color schemes, symmetry
- **Reflect deeper**: How does this connect to career pathways?

### **For English Language Learners**

- **Visual emphasis**: Focus on images over text
- **Provide examples** of collages
- **Allow native language** in text elements
- **Buddy support** from bilingual peers
- **Use quote library** instead of writing own
- **Scaffold About Me** with sentence starters

### **For Students with Disabilities**

- **Screen reader compatible** (basic support)
- **Keyboard navigation** available
- **Larger text options** in builder
- **Extended time** accommodations
- **Alternative output**: Can describe collage verbally
- **Simplified version**: Focus on 3 challenges instead of 5

## 🎨 Extension Activities

### **1. Collage Presentation (10 min)**
Students present their collage to small groups:
- 2-minute overview
- Highlight 2-3 key elements
- Share one surprising discovery

### **2. Career Connection Map**
Students create a chart:
- Column 1: Elements from collage
- Column 2: Related skills
- Column 3: Career pathways

### **3. Goal Setting**
Based on "Future Vision" challenge:
- Set 3 SMART goals
- Identify steps to achieve them
- Timeline for next 1-2 years

### **4. Collage Evolution**
Revisit quarterly:
- What has changed?
- What has stayed the same?
- Update collage with new goals

### **5. Peer Gallery & Feedback**
- Display collages (digital or printed)
- Students leave positive sticky notes
- Find 3 common themes across class

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### **Student Questions**

**Q: Do I have to complete all 5 challenges?**
A: Yes, all 5 are required to complete the activity and earn all badges.

**Q: Can I change my collage after submitting?**
A: You can edit in the builder anytime, but once you download the PDF, those are your results at that moment.

**Q: What if I don't like any of the suggested quotes?**
A: Browse the quote library (60+ quotes) or write your own!

**Q: How many images should I add?**
A: Minimum 2-3 per challenge, but quality matters more than quantity.

**Q: Can I use photos of myself or friends?**
A: Yes, but no private information and school-appropriate only.

### **Instructor Questions**

**Q: Is this activity graded?**
A: That's up to you! Can be completion-based, rubric-based, or ungraded reflection.

**Q: What if we don't have 1:1 devices?**
A: Station rotation, pairs on one device, or computer lab time work well.

**Q: Can students complete this at home?**
A: Yes! Share the link. Progress auto-saves.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: No, it's web-based but works on tablets.

**Q: What data is collected?**
A: Only what students enter (themes, interests, etc.) and their completed collage. Consult your IT department for specific privacy policies.

**Q: Can I see all student collages?**
A: Currently, students download individually. Check with your platform administrator for bulk viewing options.
